Breeder

You run a breeding lab. In each level you get a few animals or plants. You can see what they look like (phenotype), but most of their genotypes are hidden. Choose parents, breed them, and use the babies to figure out the genotypes. Reach the goal before you run out of generations.

Six levels: complete dominance, a test cross, incomplete dominance, codominance, a sex-linked trait, and multiple alleles. Fewer generations = more stars.
