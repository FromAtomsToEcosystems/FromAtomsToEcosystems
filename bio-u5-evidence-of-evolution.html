Evidence of Evolution

Why would a whale have hip bones? Look at fossils, bones, embryos, and DNA, and find out what they say about where living things came from.

Objective

I can use fossils, homologous, analogous, and vestigial structures, embryos, and DNA or protein comparisons as evidence of common ancestry, and read a cladogram.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS4-1 Communicate scientific information that common ancestry and biological evolution are supported by multiple lines of empirical evidence.

NGSS HS-LS4-2 (supports) Evolution is explained by natural selection acting on inherited variation over many generations.
