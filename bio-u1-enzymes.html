Enzymes

Fresh pineapple turns gelatin into a runny puddle, but canned pineapple doesn't. Find out how enzymes, the protein machines in every cell, speed up reactions, and why heat can ruin them.

Objective

I can explain how enzymes speed up reactions by lowering activation energy, how a substrate fits an enzyme's active site, and how temperature, pH, and substrate amount change how fast an enzyme works.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-6 (supports) Construct and revise an explanation based on evidence for how carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen from sugar molecules may combine with other elements to form amino acids and/or other large carbon-based molecules.

Builds on Unit 1 Enzymes are proteins, one of the four macromolecules. Homeostasis keeps body temperature and pH steady, which keeps enzymes working.
