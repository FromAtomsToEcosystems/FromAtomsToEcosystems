Incomplete Dominance

Cross a red flower with a white one and get pink. Find out what happens when neither allele is fully dominant, and how to predict the offspring.

Objective

I can explain incomplete dominance, write genotypes with two capital letters, and use Punnett squares to predict the 1 : 2 : 1 offspring of a heterozygous cross.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-1 Construct an explanation for how the structure of DNA determines the structure of proteins, which carry out the essential functions of life.

NGSS HS-LS3-3 Apply concepts of statistics and probability to explain the variation and distribution of expressed traits in a population.
