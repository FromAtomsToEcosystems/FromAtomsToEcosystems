Parts of an Atom
AtomThe smallest piece of an element that still has its properties.A carbon atom
ProtonPositive (+1) particle in the nucleus; its number decides the element.Carbon has 6
NeutronParticle with no charge in the nucleus.Carbon-12 has 6
ElectronNegative (−1) particle in the cloud around the nucleus.Neutral C has 6
NucleusTiny, dense, positive center of the atom.Holds p⁺ and n⁰
Atomic numberThe number of protons.Oxygen = 8
Mass numberProtons + neutrons.Oxygen-16 = 16
Isotopes
IsotopeAtoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons.Carbon-12 and carbon-14
Isotope notationMass number top left, atomic number bottom left.¹⁴₆C
StableA nucleus that does not break down.Carbon-12
RadioactiveAn unstable nucleus that decays and gives off radiation.Carbon-14
Average Atomic Mass
Average atomic massWeighted average mass of an element’s natural isotopes.Chlorine = 35.45 amu
Atomic mass unit (amu)Unit for the mass of atoms.About 1 for a proton
Percent abundancePercent of an element’s atoms that are one isotope.Cl-35 = 75.8%
Weighted averageAn average where each value counts by its share.Grades
Mass spectrometerMachine that sorts atoms by mass and measures abundance.Bar graph of isotopes
Radioactive Decay
Radioactive decayAn unstable nucleus breaking down and giving off radiation.Uranium-238
Alpha particleHelium nucleus (2p + 2n); stopped by paper.⁴₂He
Beta particleFast electron from the nucleus; stopped by aluminum.⁰₋₁e
Gamma rayEnergy with no mass or charge; needs lead.γ
Half-lifeTime for half of a radioactive sample to decay.C-14: 5,730 years
Background radiationNatural radiation from rocks, soil, and space.Everyday dose
Fission and Fusion
FissionA heavy nucleus splits into smaller nuclei.Uranium-235
FusionLight nuclei join into a heavier nucleus.The Sun
Chain reactionNeutrons from one fission cause more fissions.Reactor core
Control rodsAbsorb neutrons to control a chain reaction.Slide in to slow it
Nuclear wasteUsed radioactive fuel that stays dangerous for a long time.Spent fuel rods
