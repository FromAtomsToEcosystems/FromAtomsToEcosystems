How to read this guide: each row is one class meeting. Lesson days follow the same plan: warm-up (5–10 min) → lesson + Cornell notes (about 40 min) → worksheet (20–30 min) → exit ticket (10 min). Each lab is its own class meeting. The 28 flex meetings are kept unassigned for retakes, reteaching, lab write-ups, and semester finals. Stations days (two per unit): Stations A comes about halfway through the unit and Stations B right before the review. Each has 8 stations (one per group, about 10 minutes each) mixing hands-on cards, data and graph analysis, and short readings, and every student fills in a reflection sheet as they rotate.
Materials on the site (updated Nov 3, 2026): ✓ done ○ not built yet. Lesson days track the lesson, Cornell notes, warm-up, exit ticket, worksheet, and slides; lab days track the hands-on lab and its matching virtual lab.
Reteach activities: every topic with a lesson now has an activity for a second day (marked in the Activity column).

Unit 3: all 4 lesson days have a full set, both labs (with virtual versions), review game, escape room, Balancing Race, Card Sort, and Molecule Builder. Still to build: Unit 1, Unit 2 Families / Valence electrons / Comparing bonds, both unit tests, the Unit 3 vocabulary list, and Units 4 to 6.
