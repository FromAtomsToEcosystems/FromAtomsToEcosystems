Unit 3 Stations B

Teacher guide: Unit 3 Stations B (conservation of mass, balancing equations, and molar mass)

Use after Day 7 (calculating molar mass). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u3-stations-b-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Station 2): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Optional hands-on add-on: a balance, a sealed zip-top bag, a little baking soda, and a small cup of vinegar at Station 1 (mass before and after mixing inside the sealed bag).
  4. Put a periodic table at Stations 6, 7, and 8.
  5. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 5 and 7 take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.
