Unit 1 Stations A

Teacher guide: Unit 1 Stations A (parts of an atom, isotopes, and average atomic mass)

Use after Day 4 (Beanium lab). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u1-stations-a-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Stations 1 and 8): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Cut cards (Station 4): cut out the 8 atom cards and put them in an envelope.
  4. Optional hands-on add-ons: a small bag of mixed trail mix and a balance at Station 6, and a periodic table at every station.
  5. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 6 and 7 (calculations) take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.
