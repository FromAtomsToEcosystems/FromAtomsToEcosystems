Reef Partners

You are restoring a coral reef. Place each organism on the reef grid. Neighbors that share a side affect each other: some help, some harm. Each time two organisms meet for the first time, figure out their relationship: mutualism, commensalism, parasitism, predation, or competition. Arrange the reef so it is as healthy as possible.
