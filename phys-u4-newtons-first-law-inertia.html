Newton’s First Law and Inertia

Push a book across a desk and it stops. Kick a soccer ball and it eventually rolls to a stop. So do moving things need a force to keep going? For 2,000 years, most people thought so. Galileo and Newton showed they were wrong.

What we are learning

Objective: I can use Newton’s first law and inertia to explain and predict the motion of objects, and draw free-body diagrams with every force pointing in the correct direction.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1: analyze data to support the claim that the net force on an object changes its motion (and zero net force means no change). NGSS SEP 2: developing and using models (free-body diagrams). CCC: cause and effect.
