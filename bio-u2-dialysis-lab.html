Dialysis Tubing Lab

Biology · Unit 2 lab

What can get through a membrane?

A bag made of dialysis tubing holds starch and glucose. It sits in a beaker of water with iodine. Watch which molecules get through the bag and which stay put, and record everything here. Your work saves after every change.

Objective

I can use evidence from a dialysis tubing model to explain how a selectively permeable membrane lets some molecules through and keeps others out.

Standard: NGSS HS-LS1-3

Plan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence that feedback mechanisms maintain homeostasis.
