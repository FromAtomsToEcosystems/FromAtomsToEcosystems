Normal Force and Applied Forces at an Angle

Pushing a heavy sled from behind is hard work, but pulling it with a rope angled up feels much easier. Today you’ll find out how pushes and pulls change the normal force.

What we are learning

Objective: I can find the normal force on flat surfaces (with extra pushes or pulls and in elevators) and break an angled force into horizontal and vertical parts.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1. NGSS SEP 5: using mathematics (vector components). CCC: cause and effect.
