Carbon Budget

You are in charge of a region's energy from the 2030s to 2100. Every decade, people need more energy. Build coal, gas, solar, wind, or nuclear plants, and plant or cut forests. Keep the lights on (two blackouts and you're fired) while keeping carbon dioxide in the air under 480 ppm. Watch how carbon moves between the air, the ocean, plants, and fossil fuels.
