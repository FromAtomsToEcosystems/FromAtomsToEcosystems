Unit 3 Stations A

Teacher guide: Unit 3 Stations A (counting atoms and the five types of reactions)

Use after Day 3 (types of reactions lab). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u3-stations-a-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Station 3): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Cut cards (Station 4): cut out the 8 equation cards and 4 type labels and put them in an envelope.
  4. Optional hands-on add-on: molecule model kits (or gumdrops and toothpicks) at Station 1 so students can build H₂O and CO₂.
  5. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 1 and 2 are quick; Station 7 takes the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.

Station 6

Combustion Clues

Reading · about 10 min

Directions

  1. Read the card with your group.
  2. Answer the reflection questions.

Burning Fuels

When a fuel made of carbon and hydrogen burns, it reacts with oxygen gas (O₂). The products are always carbon dioxide (CO₂) and water (H₂O), and the reaction releases energy as heat and light. A gas stove burns methane: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. A camping stove burns propane: C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O.

If there isn't enough oxygen, some carbon monoxide (CO), a poisonous gas, can form instead. That's why gas heaters need fresh air and homes need carbon monoxide detectors.
