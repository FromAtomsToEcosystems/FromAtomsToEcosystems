Habitat Builder

You are building sealed habitat domes for a science museum. Set the temperature, moisture, and light so every species in the dome can survive, and as many as possible can thrive. Every species has its own range of tolerance. Animals also need their food, and some species need each other. You get 3 test runs per dome.
