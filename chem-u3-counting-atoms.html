Chemistry, Unit 3: Chemical Reactions

Counting Atoms

Read chemical formulas like a chemist: subscripts, coefficients, and parentheses. Then count the atoms on both sides of a reaction and see why they always match. Nine short sections, about 40 minutes. You'll get more practice on the worksheet.

Objective

I can count the atoms of each element in a formula using subscripts, coefficients, and parentheses, match formulas to particle diagrams, and count atoms on both sides of a chemical equation.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-7. Use mathematical representations to support the claim that atoms, and therefore mass, are conserved during a chemical reaction.
