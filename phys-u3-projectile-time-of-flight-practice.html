Projectile Motion and Time of Flight: Practice

Practice what you learned in the lesson. Quick problems first, then three multi-step problems. Every answer is checked with hints. About 30 minutes.

What we are learning

Objective: I can find a projectile’s launch angle, time to the top, maximum height, time of flight, and range, on level ground and from a height.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1 (supports): describe and predict motion mathematically. CA CCSS A-CED.A.4: rearrange formulas to solve for a quantity. NGSS SEP 5: using mathematics.

    How the lesson works

    ReadShort readings with diagrams. The equations live here.
    PracticeEvery answer is checked. A wrong answer gets a hint; after 3 tries you can see the worked answer.
    EquationsAll the equations are under the Equations button at the top. Picking the right one is part of the practice.

    Parts 1–4 are quick problems. Parts 5–7 are one multi-step problem each. Up is positive, ay = −9.8 m/s². Round to one decimal place (two for times). Your score is how many you get right on the first try.
