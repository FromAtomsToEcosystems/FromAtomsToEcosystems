Complete Dominance

Two black dogs can have a yellow puppy. Find out how a dominant allele can hide a recessive one, how to predict offspring with Punnett squares, and how to read a family pedigree.

Objective

I can use Punnett squares to predict offspring ratios and percents for traits with complete dominance, explain carriers and recessive disorders, and read a pedigree to work out genotypes.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-1 Construct an explanation for how the structure of DNA determines the structure of proteins, which carry out the essential functions of life.

NGSS HS-LS3-3 (supports) Use probability (Punnett squares) to explain how traits vary and are passed on in a population.
