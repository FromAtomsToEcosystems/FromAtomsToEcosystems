Unit 2 Stations B

Teacher guide: Unit 2 Stations B (ionic bonding, covalent bonding, metallic bonding, and comparing bond types)

Use after Day 13 (properties of compounds lab). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u2-stations-b-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Stations 1 and 6): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Cut cards (Station 4): cut out the 8 molecule cards and put them in an envelope.
  4. Every station: a periodic table.
  5. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 1 and 3 take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.

Station 5

The Electron Sea

Reading · about 10 min

Directions

  1. Read about metallic bonding with your group.
  2. Answer the reflection questions.

Why Metals Are Different

In a piece of metal, the atoms give up their valence electrons into a shared "sea" of electrons that moves freely through the whole solid. The positive metal ions sit in neat layers inside this sea.

Because the electrons can flow, metals conduct electricity and heat. Because the layers can slide while the sea of electrons keeps holding them together, metals can be hammered into sheets (malleable) and pulled into wires (ductile). The moving electrons also reflect light, which makes metals shiny.
