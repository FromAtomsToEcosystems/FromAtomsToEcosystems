Endothermic and Exothermic

Test four reactions with a thermometer, label a potential energy diagram, build uphill and downhill reactions, and see how a catalyst lowers the energy hill. Nine short sections, about 40 minutes.

Objective

I can tell endothermic from exothermic reactions, read a potential energy diagram (reactants, products, activation energy, ΔH), and explain how a catalyst speeds up a reaction.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-4. Develop a model to illustrate that the release or absorption of energy from a chemical reaction system depends upon the changes in total bond energy.
