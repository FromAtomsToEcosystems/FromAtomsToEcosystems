Cellular Respiration

Every cell in your body is breaking down sugar right now to make ATP, the energy your cells spend. Follow glucose through three stages and see where the energy comes from.

Objective

I can describe what goes into and comes out of glycolysis, the Krebs cycle, and the electron transport chain, where each happens, how much ATP each makes, and how aerobic respiration compares to fermentation.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-7 Use a model to illustrate that cellular respiration is a chemical process whereby the bonds of food molecules and oxygen molecules are broken and the bonds in new compounds are formed, resulting in a net transfer of energy.

NGSS HS-LS2-3 (supports) Cycling of matter and flow of energy in aerobic and anaerobic conditions.
