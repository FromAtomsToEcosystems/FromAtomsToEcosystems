Unit 2 Stations A

Teacher guide: Unit 2 Stations A (organizing the periodic table, Bohr models, ions, and periodic trends)

Use after Day 7 (periodic trends). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u2-stations-a-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Stations 1 and 5): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Cut cards (Station 2): cut out the 8 element cards and put them in an envelope.
  4. Every station: a periodic table.
  5. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 6 and 7 (trends) take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.

Ranking trends · about 10 min

Directions
