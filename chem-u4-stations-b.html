Unit 4 Stations B

Teacher guide: Unit 4 Stations B (multi-step conversions, mole ratios, and stoichiometry)

Use after Day 9 (baking soda and vinegar lab). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u4-stations-b-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Station 3): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Optional hands-on add-ons: a bag of graham crackers, marshmallows, and chocolate squares at Station 2 (build s'mores, eat later), and a periodic table at every calculation station.
  4. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Stations 1, 5, and 6 (calculations) take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.

Station 1

Two-Bridge Problems

Multi-step calculations · about 12 min

Directions

  1. Every trip goes through moles: grams ↔ moles ↔ particles or liters.
  2. Answer the reflection questions.

2. 1.20 × 10²⁴ atoms of C = ? grams (12.01 g/mol)

Station 8

Stoichiometry Spot the Mistake

Error analysis · about 10 min

Directions

  1. Four students solved problems with the equation shown. Three made a mistake; one is correct.
  2. Answer the reflection questions.

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

A. "4.0 g of H₂ makes 4.0 g of H₂O, because the ratio is 2 : 2."

B. "4.04 g H₂ ÷ 2.02 g/mol = 2.00 mol H₂ → 2.00 mol H₂O → 2.00 × 18.02 = 36.0 g H₂O."

C. "1 mol of O₂ makes 1 mol of H₂O, because there is one O₂."

D. "2.00 mol H₂O × (1 mol / 18.02 g) = 0.111 g H₂O."
