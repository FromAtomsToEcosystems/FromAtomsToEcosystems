Ionic Bonding

Watch electrons move from metals to nonmetals, figure out how many of each atom you need, then write and name ionic compounds. Nine short sections, about 40 minutes. You'll get more practice on the worksheet.

Objective

I can show how electrons transfer from a metal to a nonmetal to form an ionic bond, use electrons given = electrons taken to write the formula, name the compound, and explain the properties of ionic compounds.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-1 and HS-PS1-3. Use the periodic table to predict properties from outer electrons, and relate the structure of a substance (a lattice of ions held by electrical attraction) to its bulk properties.
