Predators, Prey, and Biodiversity

Sea otters don't eat kelp, yet without them whole kelp forests disappear. Find out how predators, prey, and the variety of life keep ecosystems stable, and what happens when they change.

Objective

I can read a predator and prey graph, explain keystone species and trophic cascades, describe genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity, and explain how threats and disturbances change ecosystems.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS2-1 Use mathematical and computational representations to support explanations of factors that affect carrying capacity of ecosystems. HS-LS2-2 Use mathematical representations to support and revise explanations about factors affecting biodiversity and populations in ecosystems.

NGSS HS-LS2-6 Complex interactions in ecosystems keep the numbers and types of organisms relatively stable, but changing conditions may result in a new ecosystem.
