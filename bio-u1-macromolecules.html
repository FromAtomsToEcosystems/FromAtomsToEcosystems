Macromolecules

A potato, a sheet of paper, and a glucose tablet are all made of the same sugar. So why can you get energy from two of them, but not from the paper? Find out what living things are built from.

Objective

I can explain why carbon is the backbone of life, how monomers join into polymers (dehydration synthesis) and break apart (hydrolysis), and describe the monomers, elements, and jobs of carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids. I can use a food label to find macromolecules in food.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-6 Construct and revise an explanation based on evidence for how carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen from sugar molecules may combine with other elements to form amino acids and/or other large carbon-based molecules.

Crosscutting concept: Structure and function How a molecule is built (its monomers, links, and shape) decides the job it can do.
