Floating Leaf Disc Lab

Biology · Unit 2 lab

Do leaves need light to make oxygen?

You'll sink spinach leaf discs in baking soda water, put one cup under a lamp and one cup in the dark, and count how many discs float each minute. Record everything here. Your work saves after every change.

Objective

I can use data from a leaf disc experiment to explain how light affects photosynthesis and the oxygen it gives off.

Standard: NGSS HS-LS1-5

Use a model to illustrate how photosynthesis transforms light energy into stored chemical energy.
