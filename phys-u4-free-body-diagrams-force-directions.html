Free-Body Diagrams and Force Directions

Toss a ball up and it keeps rising after it leaves your hand. So what’s pushing it? Today you’ll learn to draw every force on an object, with every arrow pointing the right way.

What we are learning

Objective: I can draw a free-body diagram that shows every force on an object, each with a real source and the correct direction.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1. NGSS SEP 2: developing and using models (free-body diagrams). CCC: systems and system models.
