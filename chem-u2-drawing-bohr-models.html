Chemistry, Unit 2: Ions, Periodic Trends and Bonding

Drawing Bohr Models

Count an atom's particles, fill its shells, and draw it the class way. Nine sections, about 75 minutes.

Objective

I can draw a Bohr model for any of the first 20 elements and use it to find how many valence electrons an atom has.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-1. Use the periodic table as a model to predict the relative properties of elements based on the patterns of electrons in the outermost energy level of atoms.
