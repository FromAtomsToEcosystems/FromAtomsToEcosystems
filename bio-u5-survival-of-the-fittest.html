Survival of the Fittest

You are a bird in England, 200 years ago. Peppered moths rest on tree bark. Some are pale and speckled, some are darker. Click moths to eat them before time runs out. The moths you can't find survive and have babies that inherit their color.

Play 12 generations. Watch what happens to the moths when factory soot turns the trees black, and again when the air gets clean. The graphs on the right track the colors.
