Food webs and energy
Biology · Unit 6: Ecology · Lesson

Food Webs and Energy Pyramids

Millions of shrubs, thousands of deer, and only about a dozen adult mountain lions live in the Santa Monica Mountains. Find out how energy moves through a food web, and why so little is left at the top.

Objective

I can build food chains and food webs, use the 10% rule to calculate the energy at each trophic level, and explain why matter cycles but energy flows through an ecosystem.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS2-3 Construct and revise an explanation based on evidence for the cycling of matter and flow of energy in aerobic and anaerobic conditions.

NGSS HS-LS2-4 Use mathematical representations to support claims for the cycling of matter and flow of energy among organisms in an ecosystem.
