Ions and the Octet Rule

Find out why atoms gain or lose electrons, then predict, write, and draw the ions they make. Five short sections, about 30 minutes.

Objective

I can use the octet rule to predict whether an atom gains or loses electrons, then write the ion's symbol and charge and draw its Bohr model.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-1. Use the periodic table as a model to predict the relative properties of elements based on the patterns of electrons in the outermost energy level of atoms.
