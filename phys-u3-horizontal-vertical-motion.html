Horizontal and Vertical Motion

A ball rolling off a table moves sideways and falls at the same time. The two motions never affect each other, and that makes them easy to solve.

Objective

I can explain why horizontal and vertical motion are independent, find how long a sideways-launched object is in the air and how far it goes, and find its velocity at any moment.

Standards

NGSS HS-PS2-1 (supports) Describe and predict motion mathematically, using the effect of a constant force (gravity) on an object's motion.

CA CCSS Math A-CED.A.4 Rearrange formulas to highlight a quantity of interest (like solving d = ½gt² for t).

NGSS SEP 2 and 5 Developing and using models; using mathematics and computational thinking.
