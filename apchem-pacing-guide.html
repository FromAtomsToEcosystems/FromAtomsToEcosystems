How to read this guide: each row is one block (the "Block" column counts 1–90 across the year). Lesson blocks follow the same plan: warm-up (5–10 min) → lesson + Cornell notes (about 30 min) → worksheet with AP-style practice (about 30 min) → exit ticket (10 min). The pink tags are the CED topic numbers each block covers. Every CED topic (1.1–9.11) is taught at least once. The units follow your site’s 15 AP Chemistry units, so some CED units are split across several of them (see the crosswalk). Unit 1 ends with a quick quiz instead of a test day; Units 8, 10, and 12 (the longest) get a review day. The last 4 blocks are AP exam review.
