Essential question: How does natural selection change a population over many generations?
Phenomenon: the finches after the drought. In 1977 a drought hit Daphne Major in the Galápagos. Only large, hard seeds were left, and about 85% of the medium ground finches died. The finches born after the drought had beaks about half a millimeter deeper on average. No bird changed its own beak. So how did this happen?
