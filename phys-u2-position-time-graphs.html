Position–Time Graphs

A phone’s GPS records a student’s walk to school as a graph of position vs. time. Without words, the graph tells the whole story: walking, waiting at a crosswalk, then jogging because she’s late. Today you’ll learn to read it.

What we are learning

Objective: I can interpret position–time graphs, find velocity as the slope, and recognize what curved graphs say about acceleration.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1 (analyze data and graphs of motion); SEP 4 (analyzing data); SEP 5. CCC: patterns.
