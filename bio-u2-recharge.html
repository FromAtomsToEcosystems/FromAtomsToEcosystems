Recharge

Your cell is busy: muscles contract, pumps push ions, ribosomes build proteins. Every job costs ATP. Spending ATP turns it into ADP. The cell only has 80 ATP/ADP molecules, so you must keep recharging ADP back into ATP with cellular respiration (or fermentation if there is no oxygen).
