Fossil Dig

You are a paleontologist at a canyon where a river has exposed layers of ancient rock. You have 18 digs to find five skeletons of whale relatives. Then take them to the lab: put them in order by age, use dated volcanic ash to estimate how old they are, study how their skeletons changed, and build a family tree.
