Island Split

A rising sea cuts an island in two, splitting one population of finches. You control the environment. Each generation, play one card on each side: droughts, rainy years, forests, early rains, and more. After 12 generations the sea drops and the birds meet again. Can you turn one species into two before then, without letting either side die out? Watch out for storms that blow birds across.
