Short Lesson: Projectile Motion and Time of Flight

Last lesson, the ball rolled off the table straight sideways. Now it gets kicked, thrown, and launched at an angle. How long does it stay in the air, how high does it go, and where does it land?

What we are learning

Objective: I can find how long a projectile launched at an angle stays in the air, how high it goes, and how far it lands, and explain how the launch angle changes those.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1 (supports): describe and predict motion mathematically. CA CCSS A-CED.A.4: rearrange formulas to solve for a quantity. NGSS SEP 2 and 5: developing models, using mathematics.

Goals

    How the lesson works

    ReadShort readings with diagrams. The equations live here.
    PracticeTwo problems per section. You solve them.
    Quick checkMultiple choice. Answer correctly to unlock the next section.

    Sections 1 to 5 teach and practice. Section 6 is the final check: 5 random questions, 4 out of 5 to pass. Use ay = −9.8 m/s² and the built-in calculator. Round to one decimal place (two for times). About 20 minutes.
