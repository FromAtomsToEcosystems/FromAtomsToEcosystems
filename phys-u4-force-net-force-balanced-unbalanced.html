Force, Net Force, and Balanced vs. Unbalanced Forces

So far you described motion: how fast, how far, how long. Now the big question is why things move the way they do. Why does a soccer ball speed up when kicked, slow down on grass, or sit perfectly still? The answer is forces.

What we are learning

Objective: I can identify the forces acting on an object, draw a free-body diagram, calculate the net force, and use it to tell whether the forces are balanced or unbalanced and how the motion will change.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1: analyze data to support the claim that the net force on an object changes its motion. NGSS SEP 2 and 5: developing models (free-body diagrams), using mathematics. CCC: cause and effect.
