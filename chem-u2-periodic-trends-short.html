Periodic Trends

Predict atomic radius, ionization energy, and electronegativity, then learn the shortcut that ties all three together. Four short sections, about 20 minutes.

Objective

I can predict and explain the trends in atomic radius, ionization energy, and electronegativity, and use atomic radius to predict the other two.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-1. Use the periodic table as a model to predict the relative properties of elements based on the patterns of electrons in the outermost energy level of atoms.
