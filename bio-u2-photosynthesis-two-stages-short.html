Photosynthesis

Short version for review

A quick review: which colors of light plants use, what goes in and out of each stage, and how the two stages connect. Five short sections, about 20 minutes.

Objective

I can explain how a plant captures light energy and stores it in glucose: which colors of light its pigments absorb, what the light-dependent reactions make, and how the Calvin cycle uses those products to build sugar.

Standard

NGSS HS-LS1-5. Use a model to illustrate how photosynthesis transforms light energy into stored chemical energy.
