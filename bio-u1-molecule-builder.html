Molecule Builder

You run a molecule factory inside a cell. Orders come in: build polymers from monomers, digest polymers back into monomers, and identify the macromolecules in mystery foods using food tests.

Before each build or digest order, predict how many water molecules it takes. Building removes water at every bond (dehydration synthesis). Digesting adds water at every bond (hydrolysis).
