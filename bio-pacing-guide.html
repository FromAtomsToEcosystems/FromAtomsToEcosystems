Biology Pacing Guide

Freshman biology · California NGSS · 85–90-minute alternating block (meets every other school day)
75unit class meetings
15flex meetings (unassigned)
90class meetings planned
How to read this guide: each row is one class meeting. Lesson days follow the same plan: warm-up (5–10 min) → lesson + Cornell notes (about 40 min) → worksheet (20–30 min) → exit ticket (10 min). Each lab is its own class meeting. Day 2 rows give hard topics a second day of practice and application (more worksheet practice, data, and CER), with no new subtopics. Each unit has one reteach day before the review, left open for whatever that unit's exit tickets show students missed. The 15 flex meetings stay unassigned for retakes, extra reteaching, lab write-ups, and semester finals.
Rough calendar: Aug–Sep Unit 1 · Sep–Nov Unit 2 · Nov–Dec Unit 3 · Dec–Jan fall finals (flex) · Jan–early Mar Unit 4 · Mar–Apr Unit 5 · Apr–May Unit 6 · late May–June spring finals and review (flex).
