Cell Membrane and Transport

See how a cell decides what gets in and what gets out. Build a membrane, run diffusion and osmosis labs, and power a protein pump. Nine short sections, at your own pace.

Objective

I can explain how the cell membrane maintains homeostasis by controlling what enters and leaves the cell, and I can compare passive transport (simple diffusion, facilitated diffusion, and osmosis) with active transport.

Standard

NGSS HS-LS1-3. Plan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence that feedback mechanisms maintain homeostasis.
