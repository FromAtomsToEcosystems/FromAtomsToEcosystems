Unit 5 Reteach: Skill Stations

Find the evolution skills you still need, then practice them at hands-on stations: natural selection, selection graphs, isolation, drift and gene flow, homologous structures, and cladograms.

Teacher setup: materials and room setup

Plan for about 70 minutes

  • 8 min: Skill check (6 questions). Students tap Make my station plan.
  • 45 min: 3 or 4 stations, about 12 minutes each. Call each rotation. Students go to the stations on their plan (cap 5 per table).
  • 12 min: Show what you learned. 5 min: vocabulary games if time is left.

Room setup

  • Six tables, one per station, each with a station number sign and the materials below.
  • Printable cards are inside each station (tap the dashed "Printable" box). Print from the Teacher menu, then print only the pages with cards. Cut each set and keep it in an envelope.
  • Station 1 and 4 use beans or beads. Paper squares in two or three colors work too.
  • Grading: only the Skill check and Show what you learned count toward the grade. Station practice still gets two tries and feedback, but it is not graded, because each student does different stations.
