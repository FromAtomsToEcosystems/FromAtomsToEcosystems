Unit 6 Review Task Cards

Station review: 8 tables × 3 cards

  1. Print the task cards (4 pages, 6 cards per page) and cut on the dashed lines. Put the 3 cards for each table at that table.
  2. Students record answers on the printed answer sheet or the digital chem-u6-task-cards-answer-sheet.html (it saves, scores, and sends results to the Google Sheet).
  3. Groups spend about 5 minutes per table, then rotate (Table 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1). All 8 tables take about 40 minutes.
  4. Tables: 1 Specific Heat · 2 Calorimetry · 3 Energy Diagrams · 4 Reaction Rates · 5 Le Chatelier · 6 Solubility · 7 Molarity and Dilutions · 8 Acids and Bases.
  5. The answer key is behind the teacher code.
