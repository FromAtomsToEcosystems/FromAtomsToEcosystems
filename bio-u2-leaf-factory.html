Leaf Factory

You run the chloroplasts in a leaf for one day. Use the light reactions (light + water) to make ATP and NADPH, then run the Calvin cycle (ATP + NADPH + CO₂) to build glucose. Open the stomata to let CO₂ in, but watch out: open stomata let water escape.

Goal: make 8 glucose before night, without letting the leaf wilt. The weather will change!
