Sealed Jar

A sealed jar holds water, BTB indicator, water plants, and snails. Nothing can get in or out. For 10 days, choose how many hours of light the jar gets and adjust the plants and snails so the oxygen and carbon dioxide stay safe. Each day, predict the BTB color first.

BTB is blue with little CO₂, green with some, and yellow with lots.
