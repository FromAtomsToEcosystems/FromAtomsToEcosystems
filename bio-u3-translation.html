Biology · Unit 3: DNA and Protein Synthesis · Lesson

Translation

Bacteria can read a human gene and make human insulin. Find out how ribosomes read mRNA three letters at a time to build a protein.

Objective

I can explain how the codons in mRNA are translated into a chain of amino acids at a ribosome, and how a mutation can change a protein.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-1 Construct an explanation for how the structure of DNA determines the structure of proteins, which carry out the essential functions of life.

NGSS HS-LS3-1 / HS-LS3-2 (supports) The order of bases in DNA codes for the order of amino acids in proteins; changes in DNA can change traits.
