Organelle Factory

A cell is a factory, and you are the manager. Orders come in: ship a protein, make lipids, recycle old parts, make sugar. Fill each order by clicking the right cell parts in the right order before time runs out. Every job uses ATP, so keep the power plant running. Level 1 has labels. After that, you are on your own.
