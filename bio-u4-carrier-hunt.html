Carrier Hunt

You are a genetic counselor. Each family wants to know who carries a disease allele. Use the family tree to work out every person's genotype. If more than one genotype fits the evidence, say "Can't tell." You have two DNA tests per family to settle the hardest cases. In the last two families, you must first figure out how the trait is inherited.
