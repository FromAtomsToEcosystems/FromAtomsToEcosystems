Cell Gatekeeper

You control a cell membrane. Molecules drift toward it from outside (top) and inside (bottom). For the highlighted one, choose how it crosses before it gets stuck. The small copies on each side show where there is more of it.

Active transport, endocytosis, and exocytosis cost ATP. Bringing in glucose, oxygen, and food makes more ATP (cellular respiration). Wrong choice or a stuck molecule = lose a heart.
