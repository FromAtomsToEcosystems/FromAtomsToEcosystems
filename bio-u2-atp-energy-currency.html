ATP: The Energy Currency

Ground-up firefly tails stop glowing. Add sugar: still dark. Add ATP: they light up. Find out why every cell on Earth "pays" for its work with ATP.

Objective

I can describe the parts of ATP, explain that its energy is stored in chemical bonds, show how ATP and ADP cycle as energy is spent and recharged, and explain why ATP is called the cell's energy currency.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-7 Cellular respiration is a chemical process in which the bonds of food molecules are broken and new compounds form, resulting in a net transfer of energy.

Supports: energy is stored in chemical bonds and released when they break; cells use ATP to power their processes.
