Phase Diagrams

Chemistry, Unit 5: States of Matter

Phase Diagrams

Name the six phase changes, label a phase diagram, move a dot through solid, liquid, and gas, and find out why dry ice never makes a puddle. Nine short sections, about 40 minutes.

Objective

I can name phase changes, read a phase diagram (regions, lines, triple point, critical point), and compare the phase diagrams of water and carbon dioxide.

Standard

NGSS HS-PS1-3. Plan and conduct an investigation to gather evidence to compare the structure of substances at the bulk scale to infer the strength of electrical forces between particles. HS-PS3-2 (energy of particle motion).
