Population Growth

In 1944, about 29 reindeer were left on a far-away island in Alaska. By 1963 there were about 6,000. A few years later, only about 42 were alive. Find out how populations grow, why they stop growing, and why some crash.

Objective

I can use births, deaths, immigration, and emigration to calculate population change and growth rate, compare exponential (J curve) and logistic (S curve) growth, and explain how carrying capacity and density-dependent and density-independent limiting factors control a population.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS2-1 Use mathematical and/or computational representations to support explanations of factors that affect carrying capacity of ecosystems at different scales.

NGSS HS-LS2-2 Use mathematical representations to support and revise explanations based on evidence about factors affecting biodiversity and populations in ecosystems of different scales.
