Mixed Force Problems and Applications

A tow truck pulls a car with a cable angled upward. To predict how the car moves, you need almost everything from this unit. Today you’ll put all three of Newton’s laws together.

What we are learning

Objective: I can decide which of Newton’s laws applies, and solve multistep problems with angled forces, friction, and motion.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1. NGSS SEP 5: using mathematics and computational thinking. CCC: cause and effect; systems and system models.
