Speciation

Two squirrels on opposite sides of the Grand Canyon look clearly different. Find out how one population can split into two, and how isolation, chance, and moving alleles shape new species.

Objective

I can define a species and a gene pool, explain how reproductive isolation (geographic, behavioral, temporal) leads to new species, and explain how genetic drift and gene flow change gene pools.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS4-2 Evolution results from the potential for a species to increase in number, heritable variation, competition, and selection. HS-LS4-4 Natural selection leads to adaptation of populations.

NGSS HS-LS4-5 Changes in environmental conditions may result in changes in the number of individuals of some species and the emergence of new species over time.
