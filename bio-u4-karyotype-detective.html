Karyotype Detective

You work in a genetics lab. For each patient, the lab computer has sorted some chromosomes into a karyotype, but the smaller ones are still loose. Tap a loose chromosome, then tap the spot where its pair belongs. Match by size, centromere position, and band pattern. Then count the chromosomes and make a diagnosis.
