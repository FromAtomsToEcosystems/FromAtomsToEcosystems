Lynx and Hare

You run a fur trading post in northern Canada for 30 years. Lynx hunt snowshoe hares, and both populations rise and fall. Each year, decide how many pelts to order from trappers. Lynx pelts are worth more, but if you order too many at the wrong time, the lynx disappear and the post closes. Earn bonus points by predicting which way each population will go next year.
