Unit 5 Stations A

Teacher guide: Unit 5 Stations A (particles and temperature, vapor pressure, and phase diagrams)

Use after Day 3 (phase diagrams). 8 stations, one per group of 4, about 10 minutes each. Students carry the paper reflection sheet or use chem-u5-stations-a-reflection.html on a Chromebook (it saves and sends scores to the Google Sheet).

  1. Print the station pages (one per table) and the card pages.
  2. Fold-over cards (Stations 1 and 7): cut out each card, fold on the dashed line so the answer hides behind the question, and tape the fold.
  3. Optional hands-on add-ons: a cup of hot and a cup of ice water with a drop of food coloring in each at Station 3 (the color spreads faster in hot water), and a piece of dry ice in an insulated cup at Station 8 (teacher-handled, gloves only).
  4. Rotation: 1 → 2 → … → 8 → 1, about 10 minutes each. Station 2 (Kelvin math) and Station 4 (graph reading) take the longest.

The answer key is behind the teacher code.
