Natural Selection

After a drought, the finches on one small island had deeper beaks. No bird changed its own beak. Find out how a whole population changes over generations by natural selection.

Objective

I can explain how variation, overproduction, competition, and inheritance lead to natural selection, and I can identify directional, stabilizing, and disruptive selection from graphs.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS4-2 Evolution results from (1) the potential for a species to increase in number, (2) inherited genetic variation, (3) competition for limited resources, and (4) the survival and reproduction of the organisms best able to live in the environment.

NGSS HS-LS4-3 Use statistics and probability to explain why organisms with an advantageous heritable trait tend to increase in proportion in a population.

NGSS HS-LS4-4 Construct an explanation based on evidence for how natural selection leads to adaptation of populations.
