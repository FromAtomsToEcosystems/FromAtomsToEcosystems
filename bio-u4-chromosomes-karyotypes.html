Chromosomes and Karyotypes

Two meters of DNA fit inside every tiny nucleus. Find out how DNA is packed into chromosomes, how they come in pairs, and how doctors read a picture of them called a karyotype.

Objective

I can describe how DNA is packed into chromosomes, explain homologous pairs and sex chromosomes, and read a karyotype to find the number of chromosomes, the sex, and extra or missing chromosomes.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-1 Construct an explanation for how the structure of DNA determines the structure of proteins, which carry out the essential functions of life.

NGSS HS-LS3-2 (supports) Inheritable genetic variations can come from errors as sex cells form (for example, nondisjunction).
