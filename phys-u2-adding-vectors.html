Adding Vectors with Components

Break every arrow into an x-part and a y-part, add the parts, then rebuild the answer as a right triangle.

Objective

I can break vectors into x- and y-components, add the components to find the resultant, and use the Pythagorean theorem and inverse tangent to find the resultant's length and angle.

Standards

CA CCSS Math N-VM.B.4b Given two vectors in magnitude and direction form, determine the magnitude and direction of their sum.

CA CCSS Math G-SRT.C.8 Use trig ratios and the Pythagorean theorem to solve right triangles.

NGSS SEP 5 Using mathematics and computational thinking (supports motion analysis in HS-PS2-1).
