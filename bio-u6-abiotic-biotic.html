Abiotic and Biotic Factors

During California's 2012 to 2016 drought, millions of Sierra Nevada pine trees turned red and died. Find out how the living and nonliving parts of an ecosystem limit and change each other.

Objective

I can describe the levels of organization, sort biotic and abiotic factors, tell habitat from niche, use a range of tolerance graph, and explain how limiting factors and one change can ripple through an ecosystem.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS2-1 Use mathematical and/or computational representations to support explanations of factors that affect carrying capacity of ecosystems at different scales.

NGSS HS-LS2-2 Use mathematical representations to support and revise explanations based on evidence about factors affecting biodiversity and populations in ecosystems of different scales.
