Food Web Survival

You are the ranger for a patch of California chaparral. For 10 seasons, something happens each season: a drought, a wildfire, a disease, an invasive weed. Predict how other species will change, then run the season and see if you were right. Right predictions earn eco points you can spend to help struggling species.

Read the food web: arrows point from the food to the eater. If an animal's food goes down, it usually goes down. If its predators go down, it usually goes up.

If any species drops to zero, the game is over. Keep them all alive!
