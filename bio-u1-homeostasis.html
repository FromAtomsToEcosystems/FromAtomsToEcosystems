Homeostasis

A marathon runner works hard for hours on a 32 °C day, yet her body temperature stops rising and stays near 39 °C. Find out how feedback loops keep the inside of your body stable.

Objective

I can describe homeostasis and set points, name the five parts of a feedback loop, explain negative feedback for body temperature, blood sugar, and water, explain positive feedback, and describe what happens when homeostasis fails.

Standards

NGSS HS-LS1-3 Plan and conduct an investigation to provide evidence that feedback mechanisms maintain homeostasis.

Builds on Unit 1 macromolecules Glucose is a simple sugar (a carbohydrate), glycogen is how the liver stores it, and insulin is a protein.
