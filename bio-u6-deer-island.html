Deer Island

Based on a true story. In 1944, 29 reindeer were left on St. Matthew Island in Alaska. There were no predators and lots of lichen to eat. You manage the island for 30 years. Each year you can set a hunting level, and you can bring in wolves. Earn points for a big herd, but only while the lichen stays healthy (50% or more). Can you avoid what really happened?
