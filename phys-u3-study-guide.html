Physics Unit 3 Study Guide

20 problems in 11 parts: quick vector and motion problems, concept checks, then eight multi-step problems, one per part. For every calculation you’ll pick the equation, replace the letters with numbers, then solve. About 60–70 minutes.

What we are learning

Objective: I can add vectors and break them into components, and solve horizontal-launch and projectile problems, on level ground and from a height.

Standards: NGSS HS-PS2-1 (supports): describe and predict motion mathematically. CA CCSS A-CED.A.4: rearrange formulas to solve for a quantity. NGSS SEP 5: using mathematics.

    How the lesson works

    ReadShort readings with diagrams. The equations live here.
    PracticeEvery answer is checked. A wrong answer gets a hint; after 3 tries you can see the worked answer.
    EquationsAll the equations are under the Equations button at the top. Picking the right one is part of the practice.

    In a Solve it box, type ? in the box for the value you’re solving for. Up is positive, so ay = −9.8 m/s², and anything moving or pointing down is negative. Round to one decimal place (two for times). Your score is first-try correctness, with partial credit.
